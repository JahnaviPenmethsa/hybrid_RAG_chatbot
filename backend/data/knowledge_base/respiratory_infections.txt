--- COMMON COLD ---

The common cold is a viral upper respiratory tract infection, most often caused by rhinoviruses.

Symptoms:
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Sore throat
- Mild cough
- Low-grade fever (rare in adults)
- Sneezing
- Mild headache
- Fatigue

Duration: 7–10 days

Treatment:
- Rest and hydration
- Saline nasal sprays for congestion
- Throat lozenges for sore throat
- Paracetamol for fever/pain
- Antihistamines for runny nose (first-generation)
- No antibiotics required (viral cause)

--- ACUTE BRONCHITIS ---

Inflammation of the bronchial tubes, usually caused by viral infection.

Symptoms:
- Persistent cough (can last 2–3 weeks)
- Production of mucus (clear, white, yellow, or green)
- Shortness of breath
- Chest tightness
- Wheezing
- Mild fever
- Fatigue

Treatment:
- Rest and hydration
- Honey and warm water for cough
- Cough suppressants for dry, disturbing cough
- Bronchodilators if wheezing (consult doctor)
- Antibiotics not routinely indicated

--- PNEUMONIA ---

Pneumonia is an infection of the lungs, which can be bacterial, viral, or fungal.

Symptoms:
- Productive cough (may contain blood-tinged mucus)
- High fever with chills
- Shortness of breath
- Chest pain (worsens with breathing or coughing)
- Rapid breathing
- Fatigue and weakness
- Confusion (especially in elderly)

Seek urgent medical care for pneumonia symptoms.

Diagnosis: Chest X-ray, sputum culture, blood tests

Treatment:
- Antibiotics (bacterial pneumonia — azithromycin, amoxicillin)
- Antivirals (viral pneumonia)
- Oxygen therapy (if oxygen saturation < 94%)
- Hospitalization often required

--- COVID-19 ---

COVID-19 is caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. It can range from mild to severe.

Common Symptoms:
- Fever or chills
- Dry cough
- Fatigue
- Loss of taste or smell
- Headache
- Sore throat
- Shortness of breath (moderate-severe)
- Body aches

Emergency Warning Signs (Call emergency services):
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Persistent chest pain
- Confusion or inability to wake
- Bluish lips or face

Prevention: Vaccination, masking, hand hygiene, ventilation

--- ASTHMA EXACERBATION ---

Asthma is a chronic condition with episodes of airway narrowing.

Acute symptoms:
- Wheezing
- Shortness of breath
- Chest tightness
- Cough (especially at night)

Management:
- Short-acting bronchodilator (salbutamol inhaler) for immediate relief
- Seek emergency care if no improvement after 2 puffs
- Oral corticosteroids for severe exacerbations (prescribed)
