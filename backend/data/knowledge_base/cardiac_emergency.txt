--- HEART ATTACK (MYOCARDIAL INFARCTION) ---

A heart attack occurs when blood flow to the heart is blocked, causing heart muscle damage. This is a life-threatening emergency.

Warning Signs of Heart Attack:
- Chest pain or pressure (often described as squeezing, tightness, or crushing)
- Pain radiating to left arm, shoulder, neck, jaw, or back
- Shortness of breath
- Cold sweats
- Nausea and vomiting
- Lightheadedness or dizziness
- Sudden fatigue
- Palpitations

⚠️ EMERGENCY: Call 112 (India) or go to nearest emergency room immediately.

Note: Women may present atypically — more nausea, back pain, jaw pain, fatigue without chest pain.

First Aid:
- Call emergency services immediately
- Chew aspirin 300mg (if not allergic and not contraindicated)
- Keep patient calm and still
- Loosen tight clothing
- Begin CPR if unconscious and not breathing

--- ANGINA PECTORIS ---

Chest pain caused by reduced blood flow to the heart, usually triggered by exertion.

Symptoms:
- Chest pain, pressure, or tightness
- Usually triggered by physical activity or stress
- Relieved by rest (stable angina) or occurs at rest (unstable angina)
- May radiate to arm, jaw, or shoulder
- Shortness of breath

Treatment:
- Sublingual nitroglycerine for acute episodes (prescribed)
- Beta-blockers, calcium channel blockers (chronic management)
- Lifestyle: quit smoking, reduce cholesterol, control blood pressure

Unstable angina = Emergency — seek care immediately.

--- HYPERTENSIVE URGENCY / EMERGENCY ---

Severely elevated blood pressure (> 180/120 mmHg).

Symptoms:
- Severe headache
- Shortness of breath
- Chest pain
- Nausea/vomiting
- Vision changes
- Confusion or altered consciousness

⚠️ Seek emergency care immediately.

--- CARDIAC ARRHYTHMIAS ---

Irregular heart rhythms that can cause symptoms.

Symptoms:
- Palpitations (fluttering, racing heart)
- Dizziness
- Shortness of breath
- Chest discomfort
- Fainting or near-fainting

Common types:
- Atrial fibrillation (irregular, rapid)
- Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT)
- Bradycardia (slow heart rate)

Treatment depends on type — requires ECG diagnosis and medical management.

--- PULMONARY EMBOLISM ---

Blood clot in the lungs.

Symptoms:
- Sudden shortness of breath
- Chest pain (sharp, worsens with breathing)
- Rapid heart rate
- Coughing up blood
- Leg pain/swelling (if DVT present)
- Dizziness or fainting

⚠️ EMERGENCY — Call 112 immediately.

Risk factors: Prolonged immobility, surgery, obesity, prior clots, oral contraceptives

Treatment: Anticoagulants (heparin, warfarin), thrombolytics in severe cases
