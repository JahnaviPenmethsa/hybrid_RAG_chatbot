--- URTICARIA (HIVES) ---

Urticaria is an itchy skin rash characterized by raised, red welts (wheals).

Causes:
- Allergic reactions (food, drugs, insect stings)
- Infections
- Physical triggers (cold, heat, pressure)
- Autoimmune conditions

Symptoms:
- Raised, red, itchy welts on skin
- Can appear anywhere on the body
- Individual welts typically resolve within 24 hours
- May be associated with angioedema (swelling of lips, eyes, throat)

⚠️ Seek emergency care if throat swelling or difficulty breathing (anaphylaxis).

Treatment:
- Antihistamines (cetirizine, loratadine, fexofenadine) — first line
- Oral corticosteroids (short course) for severe cases
- Epinephrine (adrenaline) auto-injector for anaphylaxis

--- CONTACT DERMATITIS ---

Inflammatory skin reaction from direct contact with an irritant or allergen.

Symptoms:
- Red rash at contact site
- Itching, burning, or stinging
- Blisters (in severe cases)
- Dry, cracked skin

Common triggers:
- Metals (nickel), soaps, detergents
- Cosmetics, perfumes
- Plants (Parthenium, poison ivy)

Treatment:
- Identify and avoid trigger
- Hydrocortisone cream (mild-moderate)
- Antihistamines for itching
- Moisturizers and emollients

--- CHICKENPOX (VARICELLA) ---

A highly contagious viral disease caused by Varicella-Zoster Virus (VZV).

Symptoms:
- Fever
- Fatigue and malaise
- Characteristic rash: starts as red spots → fluid-filled blisters → crusts
- Intense itching
- Rash progresses from trunk to face and limbs

Management:
- Calamine lotion for itching
- Antihistamines (oral) for itching
- Acyclovir (antiviral) for high-risk groups
- Keep nails short to prevent scratching
- Isolate until all lesions have crusted

--- JAUNDICE ---

Yellowing of skin and eyes due to elevated bilirubin levels.

Symptoms:
- Yellow skin and sclera (whites of eyes)
- Dark urine (tea-colored)
- Pale stools
- Fatigue
- Itching
- Abdominal pain (if liver disease)

Causes:
- Hepatitis A, B, C
- Gallstones
- Liver cirrhosis
- Hemolytic anemia

Diagnosis: Liver function tests (LFT), bilirubin levels, liver ultrasound, hepatitis serology

⚠️ Always seek medical evaluation — jaundice is a sign of underlying disease.

--- ANAPHYLAXIS ---

A severe, life-threatening allergic reaction.

Symptoms (develop within minutes of exposure):
- Throat tightening or swelling
- Difficulty breathing
- Rapid heart rate
- Severe skin rash
- Vomiting
- Dizziness or fainting
- Loss of consciousness

⚠️ EMERGENCY: Inject epinephrine (EpiPen) immediately and call 112.

Triggers: Food (peanuts, shellfish), bee stings, medications (penicillin), latex
