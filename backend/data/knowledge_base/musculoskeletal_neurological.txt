--- MALARIA ---

Malaria is a life-threatening disease caused by Plasmodium parasites, transmitted through Anopheles mosquito bites. Endemic in India.

Symptoms:
- Cyclical fever with chills (every 48–72 hours depending on type)
- Severe headache
- Muscle and joint pain
- Nausea and vomiting
- Fatigue
- Sweating after fever breaks
- Anemia (in chronic cases)

Types prevalent in India:
- P. vivax (most common, less severe)
- P. falciparum (severe, potentially fatal)

Diagnosis: Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT), peripheral blood smear, PCR

Treatment:
- P. vivax: Chloroquine + Primaquine
- P. falciparum: Artemisinin-based Combination Therapy (ACT) — artemether-lumefantrine

⚠️ Severe malaria = Emergency. Signs: altered consciousness, respiratory distress, severe anemia, renal failure.

Prevention: Insecticide-treated bed nets, indoor spraying, antimalarial prophylaxis for high-risk areas.

--- TYPHOID FEVER ---

Caused by Salmonella typhi, spread through contaminated food and water.

Symptoms:
- Prolonged fever (stepwise rise over 1 week, reaching 39–40°C)
- Severe headache
- Abdominal pain and distension
- Loss of appetite
- Constipation (early) then diarrhea
- Rose spots on trunk (faint pink macules)
- Weakness and fatigue
- Relative bradycardia (slow pulse despite high fever)

Diagnosis: Blood culture (gold standard), Widal test, Typhidot

Treatment:
- Ceftriaxone (IV) or azithromycin (oral) — for sensitive strains
- Fluoroquinolones (ciprofloxacin) — where sensitivity confirmed

--- ARTHRITIS / JOINT PAIN ---

Joint pain can be caused by many conditions.

Rheumatoid Arthritis:
- Symmetric joint involvement (both sides)
- Morning stiffness > 1 hour
- Swelling, warmth, tenderness in joints
- Small joints first (fingers, wrists)
- Fatigue, fever

Osteoarthritis:
- Joint pain with activity, relieved by rest
- Affects weight-bearing joints (knees, hips)
- Morning stiffness < 30 minutes
- No systemic inflammation

Management:
- NSAIDs (ibuprofen) for pain relief
- Disease-modifying drugs (methotrexate) for RA
- Physiotherapy and exercise
- Weight management

--- MIGRAINE ---

A primary headache disorder with moderate-to-severe throbbing pain.

Symptoms:
- Throbbing, pulsating headache (often unilateral)
- Nausea and vomiting
- Sensitivity to light (photophobia) and sound (phonophobia)
- Aura: visual disturbances, tingling (in some patients)
- Duration: 4–72 hours

Triggers: Stress, sleep changes, hormonal changes, certain foods (cheese, wine, chocolate), bright lights

Acute treatment:
- Triptans (sumatriptan) — most effective
- NSAIDs (ibuprofen, aspirin)
- Antiemetics (metoclopramide) if vomiting

Preventive treatment (if > 4 attacks/month):
- Beta-blockers (propranolol)
- Topiramate
- Amitriptyline

--- STROKE ---

A medical emergency caused by interruption of blood supply to the brain.

Recognize Stroke — FAST:
- F: Face drooping (uneven smile)
- A: Arm weakness (one arm drifts down)
- S: Speech difficulty (slurred, confused)
- T: Time to call 112

Additional symptoms:
- Sudden numbness in face, arm, leg (especially one side)
- Sudden confusion
- Vision problems
- Severe headache with no known cause
- Loss of balance or coordination

⚠️ EMERGENCY: Every minute counts. Clot-busting treatment (tPA) must be given within 4.5 hours.
