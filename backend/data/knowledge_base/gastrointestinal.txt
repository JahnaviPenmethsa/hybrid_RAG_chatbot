--- ACUTE GASTROENTERITIS ---

Gastroenteritis is inflammation of the stomach and intestines, typically caused by viral (norovirus, rotavirus) or bacterial infection.

Symptoms:
- Nausea and vomiting
- Diarrhea (watery or loose stools)
- Abdominal cramps and pain
- Low-grade fever
- Fatigue
- Loss of appetite
- Headache

Duration: 1–3 days (viral), up to 5–7 days (bacterial)

Treatment:
- Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS): Gold standard. WHO-recommended ORS composition: 2.6g NaCl, 2.9g sodium citrate, 1.5g KCl, 13.5g glucose per liter of water.
- Drink small sips frequently to prevent vomiting
- BRAT diet: Bananas, Rice, Applesauce, Toast
- Zinc supplementation for children (20mg/day for 10 days)
- Probiotics (Lactobacillus) may reduce duration
- Antibiotics only if bacterial infection confirmed (azithromycin, ciprofloxacin)

Danger Signs — Seek Medical Attention:
- Blood or mucus in stool
- High fever (> 39°C)
- Signs of dehydration: dry mouth, no urination, sunken eyes, dizziness
- Persistent vomiting preventing oral rehydration
- Diarrhea lasting more than 3 days

--- FOOD POISONING ---

Caused by consuming contaminated food with bacteria (Salmonella, E. coli, Staphylococcus) or their toxins.

Symptoms (onset: 1–72 hours after contaminated food):
- Sudden nausea
- Vomiting
- Diarrhea (may be bloody)
- Abdominal cramps
- Fever
- Weakness

High-risk foods in India:
- Undercooked meat and poultry
- Raw or improperly handled dairy
- Leftover rice (Bacillus cereus)
- Street food with poor hygiene

Treatment: Similar to gastroenteritis (ORS, rest, BRAT diet)

--- IRRITABLE BOWEL SYNDROME (IBS) ---

IBS is a chronic functional disorder affecting the large intestine.

Symptoms:
- Abdominal pain or cramping (often relieved after bowel movement)
- Bloating
- Alternating constipation and diarrhea
- Mucus in stool
- Gas

Management:
- High-fiber diet
- Avoid trigger foods (dairy, fatty foods, alcohol, caffeine)
- Stress management
- Antispasmodics (mebeverine) for cramps
- Loperamide for diarrhea-predominant IBS
- Fiber supplements (ispaghula husk) for constipation-predominant IBS

--- PEPTIC ULCER DISEASE ---

Sores in the lining of the stomach or upper small intestine.

Symptoms:
- Burning stomach pain (often between meals)
- Nausea
- Bloating
- Heartburn
- Loss of appetite
- Dark or bloody stools (severe)

Causes: H. pylori infection, NSAIDs, stress

Treatment:
- Proton pump inhibitors (omeprazole, pantoprazole)
- H. pylori eradication therapy if confirmed (triple therapy: PPI + 2 antibiotics)
- Avoid NSAIDs, alcohol, smoking
