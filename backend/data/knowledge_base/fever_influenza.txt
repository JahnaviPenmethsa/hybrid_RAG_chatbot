--- FEVER ---

Fever is defined as a body temperature above 38°C (100.4°F). It is a common sign of infection and the immune system's response to pathogens.

Common Causes of Fever:
- Viral infections: influenza, common cold, dengue, COVID-19
- Bacterial infections: typhoid, urinary tract infection, pneumonia
- Other: heat exhaustion, autoimmune conditions, drug reactions

Symptoms typically associated with fever:
- Body temperature above 38°C
- Chills and shivering
- Sweating
- Headache
- Muscle pain and body aches
- Fatigue and weakness
- Loss of appetite

Management of Fever:
- Rest and hydration (oral rehydration salts if needed)
- Paracetamol (acetaminophen): 500–1000mg every 4–6 hours for adults (do not exceed 4g/day)
- Ibuprofen (if no contraindications): 400mg every 8 hours
- Tepid sponging for comfort
- Monitor temperature every 4 hours

Seek emergency care if:
- Temperature exceeds 40°C (104°F)
- Fever in infants under 3 months (any fever = emergency)
- Fever persists beyond 3 days
- Associated with stiff neck, rash, confusion, or difficulty breathing
- Febrile seizure occurs

--- INFLUENZA (FLU) ---

Influenza is a contagious respiratory illness caused by influenza viruses. It spreads via respiratory droplets.

Symptoms of Influenza:
- Sudden onset high fever (38–40°C)
- Severe headache
- Muscle aches and body pain
- Extreme fatigue
- Dry cough
- Sore throat
- Runny or stuffy nose
- Chills

Duration: 5–7 days for most symptoms; fatigue may last 2 weeks.

Treatment:
- Bed rest
- Adequate hydration
- Antipyretics for fever (paracetamol preferred)
- Antiviral medications (oseltamivir/Tamiflu) within 48 hours for high-risk groups
- Isolation to prevent spread

Prevention:
- Annual influenza vaccination (recommended by WHO)
- Hand hygiene
- Respiratory etiquette (covering mouth when coughing)
- Avoiding close contact with sick individuals

High-risk groups requiring early medical attention:
- Children under 5 years
- Adults over 65 years
- Pregnant women
- Immunocompromised individuals
- Persons with chronic conditions (diabetes, heart disease, asthma)
